Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

Green Jewelers ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website eligreenjewelers.com. We provide custom jewelry design, wholesale diamond sourcing, and personal consultations in Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, and online across the United States.

Contact us at: info@eligreenjewelers.com or (646) 276-3100.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when:

  • Booking a consultation or submitting our contact form — your name, phone number, email address, preferred meeting method, and design preferences.
  • Communicating with us via WhatsApp, phone, or email — your contact details, reference photos, and project details.

We also collect anonymous usage data automatically when you visit our website (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Schedule and conduct personal or online jewelry consultations.
  • Create custom jewelry designs, 3D CAD renderings, and price quotes tailored to your request.
  • Fulfill orders, arrange secure delivery, and provide warranty and aftercare support.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We treat every consultation and custom piece with strict confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Analytics and Cookies

This website uses Matomo Analytics, a self-hosted, privacy-focused analytics platform. Matomo collects anonymous data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site.
  • Referring website or search engine.
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level).
  • Device type, browser, and screen resolution.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses Google Fonts for typography. Google may log font requests in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain client consultation notes, order records, and certification details for as long as necessary to provide our services, honor warranties, and comply with legal obligations. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, as well as to opt out of analytics tracking. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@eligreenjewelers.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
